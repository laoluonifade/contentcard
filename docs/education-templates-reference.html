07 · Glossary Tech · Vector Lab
GLOSSARY · ENTRY 18 · GAN vectorlab.io · 2026

GaN

/ˈɡal.ɪ.əm ˈnaɪ.traɪd/
noun · semiconductor material

A wide-bandgap semiconductor that lets a charger move power through a much smaller silicon die — ~3× more efficient than the silicon equivalent. Less heat, less plastic, same wattage.

From gallium (Ga, the element) + nitrogen. First commercial use in chargers: 2018. Standard in fast-charge bricks since '22.

10 · Common mistake Skincare · Solbright
COMMON MISTAKE · 03 SOLBRIGHT · ESSAY · 2026

Most people use a pea-sized amount — you need a quarter-teaspoon for the face alone.

Sunscreen testing assumes 2 mg per cm² of skin. For a face, that's roughly a quarter-teaspoon — about three times what most people apply. Anything less and the SPF on the label isn't the SPF on your face.
